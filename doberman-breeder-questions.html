How to use the list

Take the page to the visit or keep it next to you on the phone, and write each answer down as you go. Questions are a way to get to know the breeder and the litter; there is no single right answer to most of them. The list covers the parents, the litter, matching and going home. It leaves out health screening and paperwork, which are better talked through with your veterinarian and read in full before you sign anything.

Once the puppy is home, the guides cover teaching a calm settle and daily exercise.
